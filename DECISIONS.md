# Decisions

- Replace only a personal maintenance ledger, not the incumbent's social network or corpus.
- Store up to three vehicles, 300 records per vehicle, and 50 reminders per vehicle locally.
- Do not collect registration plates, VINs, location, contacts, accounts, or cookies.
- Reduce each optional image to JPEG at no more than 220 KB before IndexedDB storage.
- Make `.seibito` the full editable backup and CSV/PDF the handoff formats.
- Treat due dates and odometer thresholds as user notes, not vehicle-specific safety advice.
